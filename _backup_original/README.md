# Marketing Tracker Prototype

A clean, minimalist working prototype focused strictly on the marketing workflow:
**Came (Visited) &rarr; Filled Form &rarr; Attended Quiz**.

Contains **zero dummy values** (starts completely clean).

---

## Features

1. **3 Core Metrics**:
   - **People Who Came**: Visitors / link clicks.
   - **Filled The Form**: Leads who submitted their details.
   - **Attended The Quiz**: Candidates who took the assessment quiz.

2. **Time Aggregation**:
   - **Daily View**: Day-by-day table showing Came, Filled Form, and Attended Quiz.
   - **Week-wise View**: Grouped by calendar weeks.
   - **Month-wise View**: Grouped by calendar months.

3. **Simple Filters**:
   - **Source / Channel**: Instagram, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Facebook, Email, Direct.
   - **Date Range**: All Time, Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days.

4. **Working Actions**:
   - Click **"+ Add Entry"** to record an entry with its stage, source, and date.
   - One-click stage progression: Advance an entry from **Came &rarr; Form Filled &rarr; Quiz Attended**.
   - Delete individual entries or click **Clear All** anytime.
