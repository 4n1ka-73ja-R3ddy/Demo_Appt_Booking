Technical Assessment Instructions

Candidate Examination Guidelines and Evaluation Rules

1. Structure & Format

  • The assessment contains a total of 40 questions covering four curriculum sections: Data Communication & Networks (DCN), Operating Systems (OS), Database Management Systems (DBMS), and Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA).
  • Questions 1 to 38 are adaptive theoretical Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs).
  • Questions 39 and 40 are live coding challenges evaluated against public and hidden test cases in an isolated execution runtime.
  • Questions adapt in real time across four difficulty tiers: Easy (+2 pts), Moderate (+4 pts), Hard (+6 pts), and Very Hard (+10 pts). Answering correctly advances you to higher tiers, while incorrect answers step you down and incur a -1 pt penalty. Each domain section (Q1, Q11, Q21, Q31) automatically starts at Moderate.

2. Timing & Navigation

  • You are allotted a strict 90-minute floating countdown timer that begins the moment you click Start Exam.
  • Questions are delivered sequentially through the adaptive engine; direct navigation through the question palette is disabled to maintain integrity.
  • You have a universal budget of 10 skips. Using a skip preserves your current difficulty tier and rotates to a different subtopic within that section.

3. Proctoring & Submission Rules

  • Anti-cheat mechanisms are strictly enforced: switching tabs, blurring the window, or using unauthorized shortcuts will trigger immediate proctoring strikes.
  • Your code and answers are auto-saved as you submit each question.
  • Once submitted, answers cannot be modified. The assessment will automatically conclude once Question 40 is finalized or when the timer expires.
